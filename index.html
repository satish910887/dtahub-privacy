DTAhub Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Summary: We collect only your email and a hashed password for authentication. No tracking, no ads, no data selling.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you register, we collect your email address and a securely hashed password. This is used solely for authentication and account recovery.

Usage Data: We track anonymous download counts to show popular apps. No personal identifiers are attached.

Local Data: Your favorites, recently used apps, and preferences are stored ONLY on your device. They are not transmitted to our servers.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Phone number, contacts, or SMS
  • Location data
  • Photos, camera, or microphone access
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address)
  • Browsing history
  • Financial information
  • Advertising IDs

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate your account
  • To provide app functionality (favorites, history)
  • To improve the app experience

4. Data Storage & Security

Account data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL) with industry-standard encryption. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt. We never store plain-text passwords.

5. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. We do not display third-party ads.

6. Third-Party Services

We use the following for infrastructure:

  • Railway — API hosting
  • Supabase — Database

Both comply with GDPR and industry security standards.

7. Your Rights (DPDP Act, GDPR)

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Correction: Update your information
  • Deletion: Delete your account anytime
  • Portability: Export your data
  • Withdraw Consent: Stop using the app

8. Account Deletion

Email satish910887@gmail.com with subject "Delete My Account" and your registered email. We will delete all your data within 7 days.

9. Children's Privacy

DTAhub is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
